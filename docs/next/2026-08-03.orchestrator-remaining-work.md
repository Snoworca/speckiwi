## 0. Read this first — the shape every remaining defect has

Four live defects closed on 2026-08-02 had one form:

> **The predicate is correct, it is tested, and nothing calls it.**

`hasMergeWitness` had the right answer about a landed unit while `computeResumeState` decided from a
lane branch phase 1 never creates. `validateCard` enforced ten violations and had one caller, inside
`writeCard`, so nine of them passed on the resume path. `isVerb` was consulted by nothing on either
the append or the resume path. `scanProse` still has no caller at all.

**So the first question to ask of any predicate in `src/core/orchestrator/` is not "is it right?" but
"who calls it?"** — and the answer must be measured with `grep -a`, never plain `grep`: four files
under `src/` carry literal NUL bytes (`conflict.ts` 1, `handoff.ts` 2, `resume.ts` 2,
`parser/workspace-parser.ts` 1), so `grep` prints `Binary file … matches` and no line hits. A census
run without `-a` silently under-reports and counts that one notice as a hit. That is how the original
count of 12 unwired kernels was taken, and it was wrong in two entries.

**The corrected census is 19, not 12** (`grep -a`, excluding each function's own module):

`countNormativeTokens` and `hasMergeWitness` are **not** independently unwired — they are internal

### 1.1 The gate table needs an `evaluator` column — **4 of 5 wiring members, independently**

Add a fourth column over a closed two-value vocabulary (`tool` | `agent`; the `tool` form may name
its verb or its predicate symbol), in all three variants, plus a **census test**: every `tool` row's

### 1.2 `orchestrate route probe` must PRODUCE a probe, not only consume one — **5 of 5**

`05` §10.6's verb table says the verb *"writes the probe from the declared producers (09 §3.2)"*, and
`09` §14's draft requirement says it *"writes `routing/probe.json` from the declared sources"*. The
authored `IR-CLI-082` AC-7 kept only the second clause — refuse on a schema-invalid probe — so the
shipped verb consumes `--probe`/`--payload` and echoes it. **The narrowing from produce to echo is
recorded in no requirement.** That is why the seven kernels have no caller: their caller was specified
away.

The brief's blocker — *"the CLI cannot make MCP calls"* — was measured and does not bind. The CLI has
in-process equivalents for every read `09` §3.2 names (`list --trace-reference --fields --target`,
`active-target`, `scopes`, `mode`, `workflow next-task`). Only the four subagent artifacts, `gh issue
view` and S7's 1.a classification are genuinely agent-supplied, and the precedent for exactly that
split is shipped: `orchestrate resume --facts` refuses to run without agent-supplied facts because
*"the tool never invents them"*.

**And the two copies already disagree on live input.** `09` §3.2 requires an undated plan candidate to
sort below every dated one. Of the six `docs/plans/*.plan.md`, exactly one lacks `generated_at` —
`2026-07-29.speckiwi.v243.plan.md` — and it carries the newest date **in its filename**. An agent
following the prose picks it; `selectPlanCandidate` ranks it last.

**Shape:** add `--derive <facts.json>` (or equivalent options) to the **existing** `route probe` leaf
rather than a new verb. `IR-CLI-082` AC-1 counts verb **rows** ("exactly the twenty-one"), and options
do not change the row count — a new `route derive` verb would demote both `IR-CLI-082` and
`IR-MCP-003`. The MCP mirror is free: `read-tools.ts` derives the `orchestrate_*` family from the CLI
bindings.

Do **not** delete the kernels. `FR-NODE-111/112/115/116/117/118` are `verified` on function-scoped
criteria the kernels satisfy; none makes a runtime claim, so none is dishonest today.

## 3. The one ledger decision left

`test/skills/orchestrator-mutation-ledger.json` exists: 31 rules × 3 variants = 93 rows, 66 carrier
anchors covering all 22 modal carriers per variant, every mutation applied and confirmed red, seven
anti-vacuity probes all red.

An independent audit re-derived the carrier scan and found **coverage complete and exact** — zero
uncovered carriers, every `mutation.find` resolving exactly once, and **no criterion naming a sentence
that is absent from the body**. It then answered the question the ledger cannot answer for itself:

> **The narrow reading of "normative sentence" is right, and six of the eight criteria are therefore
> vacuous.**

§10.3 defines a normative sentence mechanically — one carrying a member of the modal set — and the
criteria quote that clause verbatim. The redundancy argument for the wide reading does not hold: the
two clauses quantify over different things (per sentence vs per carrier occurrence), and §10.3 says
why. Under the narrow reading `074`, `077`, `078`, `079`, `080` and `081` add **zero** carrier-bearing
sentences.

**So the defect is in the criterion's denominator, not in the ledger.** §10.3's anti-vacuity floor is
body-scope; projecting it per requirement reintroduces at requirement scope exactly the
empty-denominator problem it solved at body scope.

**The decision to make:** tick nothing and record the criteria as inapplicable, or restate them. If
the wide reading were taken instead, the ledger is short roughly **28 sentences** across seven rows
(075 +4, 076 +3, 077 +2, 078 +5, 079 +5, 080 +4, 081 +5) — the audit does not recommend it but
priced it.

tick.**

## 5. Two things not to repeat

**A probe that replaces nothing proves nothing.** It happened twice this session: a `perl` substitution
that matched zero times left the file untouched and the suite green, and a `python` regex that missed
an `extends` clause did the same. Both were briefly read as passes. **Every probe must assert its own
guard held** — `assert old in s` before writing, and `assert new != old` after.

**A non-vacuity guard must not depend on incidental state.** `FR-NODE-158`'s first version asserted
`git status --porcelain` was non-empty, which is only true while someone has uncommitted work; it
failed the moment the tree was committed. The guard now creates its own control file. The general
form: a guard that passes because of the environment rather than because of the property is not a
guard.
