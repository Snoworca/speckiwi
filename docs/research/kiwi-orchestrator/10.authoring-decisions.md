## 2. Committee decisions

Five members, each investigating independently and none seeing another's reasoning.

### D-A3 — the confidence-spread fold supplies no multiplier

**4–1.** Four members refused to supply a de-rating factor; one proposed ×0.8.

**Decided:** §4.1.2 supplies **no** confidence adjustment of its own and no re-vote. The spread is
recorded in the decision audit row and drives nothing. The governing confidence is the minimum within
the winning bloc after §4.1.1's per-member adjustments — the whole committee when the vote is
unanimous — and escalation to `critical` fires only when that one value falls below the threshold in
force.

The refusal is the substance of the decision, not a failure to decide. §4.1.1's four constants are
asserted in the skill file and derived nowhere, so a fifth would be a fifth uncalibrated number, and
`05` records minting one as *"the fourth instance of the thing three recorded rulings refuse"*.

One member additionally found that the **replaced text contradicted an already-authored requirement**:
`FR-FLOW-068`'s old AC-2 applied a committee-wide spread adjustment, which lets a dissenting member's
confidence enter the comparison `FR-FLOW-069` AC-3 excludes it from. That contradiction, not the
missing factor, is why the criterion had to be rewritten rather than left vague.

A fifth member proposed widening the minimum to **all** members under a split vote when the spread is
wide. Three members' positions exclude it and it collides with the same `FR-FLOW-069` AC-3, so it is
not adopted.

Applied to `FR-FLOW-068` AC-2 and AC-3, with a change note.

### D-T40 — the downgrade gate's magnitude condition is deleted, and no number replaces it

**5–0.**

**Decided:** `route-downgrade-available` fires on **three** conditions. The wave's `design_items`
count is carried as gate evidence and drives no predicate — the treatment `09` §3.2 already gives
`S5.files` and `similarity_score`.

Three findings, each from a different member:

- The reference is **circular**: `09` §3.2 defers the magnitude to §7.4, and §7.4 defers it back to
  the probe. §3.2 states outright that no calibrated magnitude threshold exists anywhere in the suite.
- It is **structurally impossible**: `probe.json` is frozen at 1.c′ into `invariant_digest`, while
  `design_items` do not exist until the Phase 2 freeze. The value could not live in the probe even if
  a threshold existed.
- The nearest real constant, `kiwi-tdd`'s 200-line SDS cap, **does not transfer**: it is in SDS lines
  rather than design items, no conversion is stated, and the SDS does not exist at 2.e.

The cost of over-offering the gate is bounded at one prompt, because `continue-orchestrated` carries
the `recommended` marker, so `--auto` never downgrades on it.

Applied to `FR-FLOW-089` (Requirement, AC-1, AC-2), `FR-FLOW-103` AC-8, `09` §7.4 and `05` §1.4, each
with a change note.
